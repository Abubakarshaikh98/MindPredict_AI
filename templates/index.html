    Enter information

    Provide lifestyle, academic, social media, sleep, physical activity, and stress information.

  2. 02

    Data processing

  4. 04

    Prediction

About this project

MindPredict AI combines machine learning, Python, FastAPI, data processing and web development into a single working application — a practical demonstration of how a trained model moves from a notebook into something people can actually use.

It's an educational Machine Learning and Data Science project, built to show how a trained model can be integrated end-to-end: from a form in the browser, through an API, into a prediction and back.

Disclaimer. MindPredict AI is not a diagnostic or medical tool. It does not assess, diagnose, or treat any mental health condition. If you are struggling, please reach out to a licensed mental health professional or a crisis service in your region.
